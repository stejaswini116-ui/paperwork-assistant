# Paperwork Chief of Staff

An AI agent that reads a bill or notice, finds the deadline, amount and
required action, asks for your approval, then sets a reminder.

## Problem
Students and families miss deadlines and payments because bills, college
notices and emails are long and confusing.

## How it works
1. Paste or upload a notice.
2. The AI extracts the deadline, amount and action.
3. You approve or reject the action.
4. The app sets a reminder.

## Safety
- The agent never acts without your approval.
- If it is unsure, it asks you instead of guessing.
- Only made-up sample documents are used. No real personal data.
